## Abstract

This paper presents a comparative analysis of transformer-based neural machine translation (NMT) models applied to low-resource language pairs. mBART with targeted fine-tuning outperforms other architectures by an average BLEU score improvement of 3.2 points.

## Results

| Model | Swahili-EN | Pashto-EN | Amharic-EN |
|-------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Base Transformer | 24.7 | 18.3 | 15.1 |
| mBART | **28.4** | **21.2** | **17.8** |
| M2M-100 | 26.1 | 19.5 | 16.4 |
