## Award Details

The Graduate Research Fellowship is awarded annually to graduate students who demonstrate exceptional research potential and academic excellence. Recipients receive full tuition support and a research stipend for the duration of their master's program.

**Institution**: University of Toronto | **Value**: $25,000 CAD
