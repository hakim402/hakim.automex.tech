## Course Overview

This IBM course on Coursera teaches the Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining (CRISP-DM) — the most widely used framework for data science projects.

## Phases Covered

- **Business Understanding** — Defining objectives, success criteria, and project scope
- **Analytic Approach** — Selecting the right modeling approach based on business requirements
- **Data Requirements & Collection** — Identifying and gathering necessary data sources
- **Data Understanding & Preparation** — Exploratory data analysis, cleaning, feature engineering
- **Modeling & Evaluation** — Building, tuning, and validating models against success criteria
- **Deployment & Feedback** — Production deployment, monitoring, and iterative improvement
